Rules and privacy

Plain version. You only need this if you sign in.

What gets stored

  • A username you pick. Everyone can see it.
  • Your sign in. Discord tells the site who you are. There is no password on this site, and I never see one. Guests use no sign in at all: they get an anonymous account that lives in their browser and is deleted after 90 days.
  • Your comments on comic pages, with the date.
  • Your place in each comic, so it can open where you stopped.
  • Your game results for the Battleship leaderboard: wins and losses.

Who else touches it

  • Supabase stores accounts and data for me.
  • Discord handles the sign in if you use it. I only get what is needed to make your account.
  • GitHub hosts the site itself.
  • Online Battleship connects your browser straight to your opponent's, and that can show them your IP address. Play the bot if that bothers you.
  • The visitor counters count visits. They do not know who you are.

Comment rules

  • Be decent. No hate, no harassment, no spam, no links to sketchy stuff.
  • No personal info about anyone, including yourself.
  • I can delete any comment, and remove anyone who ruins it for others.
  • You need to be 13 or older to sign in.

Getting out

Click your username at the top of the comics or Battleship page, then Delete my account. That removes your username, comments, place in each comic, and scores for good.

If that does not work, message me on Discord and I will do it by hand.

Questions? Ask in the Discord.
