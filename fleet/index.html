Weapons

Shell1 tile, 4 damage. Every tile has 4 armor, so one shell destroys it. From Battleships and Dreadnoughts.
Machine gun4 shots, 1 damage each. Spread them out or stack them on one tile. You only learn what you hit after the whole burst. From Destroyers.
MortarOnce per match. Hits a rounded 5x5 area: every tile takes damage, the centre takes 5, plus 8 random extra hits. From Cruisers.
Sonar pingFrom Submarines. Counts ship tiles in a 3x3. No damage. Only your team sees the answer.
Recon flightFrom Carriers. Marks every ship tile in a 3x3 for your team. Recharges for 2 turns.
Bait boatsDecoys that look real. They do not count toward winning.

Armory

Win games with 2 or more real players to earn coins. Weapons from the shop are side-grades, not upgrades: each one trades something away.
