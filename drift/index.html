Drift

Petryn's card game, from The Long Shot Initiative. He swears he didn't make it up. Line up the orbit and take it home.

Table talk

    How to play

    One deck, two players. Twelve cards sit face down in a ring: the orbit. The rest of the deck is the Dark.

    • Two face-up neighbours line up if they share a suit, share a rank, or are one rank apart. King and Ace count as one apart.
    • Three or more face-up cards in a row that all line up make a Line. Lines glow blue. All one suit glows gold.
    1. You may nudge once: tap a face-up card, then tap a card next to it to swap them.
    2. Then do one thing: flip a face-down card, claim a Line (tap a card in it), or jettison a face-up card, only when nothing in the orbit is face down.
    • A Line scores 3, plus 2 for every card past three. All one suit scores double.
    • Claimed cards are replaced face down from the Dark. When the Dark runs out, the ring gets smaller.
    • Petryn's rule: you can't nudge back the two cards your opponent just nudged.
    • The game ends when nobody can flip, claim, or jettison. Most points wins.

    Flipping is the risky part. Whatever you flip, your opponent gets to see too.
